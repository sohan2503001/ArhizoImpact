Brand Narrative

Design Symbolism & Brand Architecture

Every curve in the Arhizo Impact logo tells the story of our dual mission:

1. The Golden Rhizome Roots

Stemming from the botanical definition of "Rhizome" (an underground interconnected root system), the golden base branches and micro-nodes represent community networks, rural enterprise empowerment, and resilient grassroots livelihoods.

2. The Ascending Emerald Leaf

The flourishing dual canopy leaves in vivid emerald and mint reflect environmental stewardship, afforestation, urban forestry, and natural carbon sinks reaching skyward.

3. The Net-Zero Circular Orbit

The dynamic orbital arc and golden sun node encircle the mark, signifying closed-loop circular economy, solar energy, climate balance, and global decarbonization advisory.
